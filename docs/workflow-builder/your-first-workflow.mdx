title: "Your First Workflow"
description: "Build a working WhatsApp booking workflow from scratch in under 10 minutes"
---

This guide walks you through building a real workflow: a WhatsApp booking handler that collects customer info and confirms an appointment. By the end, your AI will be able to:

- Greet incoming WhatsApp messages
- Collect the customer's name and preferred appointment time
- Book the slot on your Google Calendar
- Send a confirmation message

**Time to complete:** 8–10 minutes

---

## Before you start

Make sure you have:

- A Lynq account with a connected WhatsApp number ([set up here](/whatsapp/settings))
- A connected Google Calendar ([set up here](/integrations/google-calendar)) — optional, but needed for the booking step

---

## Step 1: Create a new workflow

1. Go to **Workflow** in the left sidebar
2. Click **Create Workflow**
3. Give it a name — e.g. "Appointment Booking"
4. Click **Create**

You'll see the workflow canvas with one block already placed: the **Trigger** block.

---

## Step 2: Configure the Trigger

Click the Trigger block to open its settings.

1. Set **Trigger type** to **Inbound WhatsApp**
2. Set **Channel** to your WhatsApp number
3. Leave the other settings as defaults

This means: whenever a customer messages your WhatsApp, this workflow starts.

Click **Save**.

---

## Step 3: Add an AI Conversation block

This block handles the actual conversation — greeting the customer and collecting their info.

1. Click the **+** button below the Trigger block
2. Select **AI** → **AI Conversation**
3. Click the new block to configure it

In the **Goal** field, enter:
```
Greet the customer warmly. Ask for their full name and preferred date and time for an appointment. Confirm once you have both.
```

In the **Persona** field, enter:
```
You are a friendly receptionist for [your business name]. Keep responses short and conversational. You are speaking over WhatsApp.
```

This is the instruction you give to the AI — it will figure out how to have the conversation naturally based on these goals. You don't need to script every message.

Click **Save**.

---

## Step 4: Add a Create Appointment block

Once the AI has collected the name and preferred time, this block books it.

1. Click **+** below the AI Conversation block
2. Select **Data** → **Create Appointment**
3. Configure:
   - **Calendar:** Select your connected Google Calendar
   - **Title:** `Appointment with {{contact.name}}`
   - **Start time:** `{{collected.preferred_datetime}}`
   - **Duration:** `30 minutes`
   - **Guest email:** `{{contact.email}}` (leave blank if you don't collect email)

<Info>
The `{{collected.preferred_datetime}}` variable is automatically populated by the AI Conversation block above — it extracts what the customer said into a structured date/time value.
</Info>

Click **Save**.

---

## Step 5: Add a Send WhatsApp block

Confirm the booking with the customer.

1. Click **+** below the Create Appointment block
2. Select **Channel** → **Send WhatsApp**
3. Enter the message:

```
Your appointment is confirmed for {{appointment.start_time_formatted}}. 
We'll see you then, {{contact.name}}! 

If you need to reschedule, just reply here.
```

Click **Save**.

---

## Step 6: (Optional) Alert your team

If you want a notification when someone books:

1. Click **+** below the Send WhatsApp block
2. Select **Notification** → **Alert Team**
3. Configure:
   - **Send via:** WhatsApp or Email
   - **To:** Your phone number or email
   - **Message:** `New appointment booked by {{contact.name}} for {{appointment.start_time_formatted}}`

---

## Step 7: Publish and test

1. Click **Publish** in the top-right corner
2. Send a test message to your WhatsApp number — something like "Hi, I'd like to book an appointment"
3. Watch the AI handle the conversation

You should see:
- AI responds with a greeting and asks for your name + time
- Once you provide both, the appointment appears in your Google Calendar
- You receive a confirmation message on WhatsApp

---

## What your workflow looks like

```
Inbound WhatsApp (Trigger)
    ↓
AI Conversation — "Collect name + preferred time"
    ↓
Create Appointment — books on Google Calendar
    ↓
Send WhatsApp — "Your appointment is confirmed..."
    ↓
Alert Team — notifies you via WhatsApp
```

5 blocks. Working 24/7. No code.

## Common issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The AI keeps asking for info it already has">
    Check the **Goal** text in your AI Conversation block. Be specific about exactly what you want collected. Example: "Collect: (1) full name, (2) preferred date, (3) preferred time. Confirm once you have all three."
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The appointment shows wrong time zone">
    Go to **Settings → Organisation → Time Zone** and make sure it matches your location. The AI interprets times in your organisation's timezone.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The confirmation message uses {{contact.name}} literally">
    Make sure the AI Conversation block comes *before* the Send WhatsApp block — variables are only populated after the AI has extracted them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I want to add a condition — only book during business hours">
    Add a **Branch** block after the Trigger. Set the condition to: "Current time is between 9am and 6pm on a weekday." If true, continue to the AI block. If false, add a **Send WhatsApp** block with an after-hours message.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
