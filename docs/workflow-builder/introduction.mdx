## What is the Workflow Builder?

The Lynq Workflow Builder is a drag-and-drop canvas where you design how your AI Employee handles customer conversations.

Think of it like a flowchart for your business process. You snap blocks together to describe what should happen — and Lynq runs it 24/7, automatically, across every channel your customers use.

## The core idea: lego blocks

Every workflow is built from the same 8 types of blocks, regardless of your industry or use case.

```
TRIGGER → AI → LOGIC → DATA → CHANNEL → INTEGRATE → NOTIFY → END
```

You don't need to understand all 8 right away. Most workflows only use 3–4 block types. Start simple and add complexity as you grow.

## Why one builder for all channels?

Before Lynq, voice and WhatsApp automation were two separate tools with two separate setups. That meant double the work and inconsistent customer experiences.

The Lynq Workflow Builder uses **one workflow** that runs across all channels. The same logic that books a resort room over WhatsApp also books it over a phone call — you build it once.

| Channel | What it means |
|---|---|
| WhatsApp | Customer sends you a message |
| Voice | Customer calls your number (or you call them) |
| Email | Customer emails you, or you send a campaign |
| SMS | Short text messages for reminders and alerts |

## What you can build

A few examples of what businesses use Lynq workflows for:

- **Hotel booking** — Guest messages "I want a room for 2 nights," AI checks availability, quotes price, confirms booking, sends reminder the day before
- **Real estate lead qualification** — WhatsApp ad lead comes in, AI qualifies budget and timeline, books site visit, alerts your sales team
- **Clinic appointment booking** — Patient calls after hours, AI takes their name and preferred time, books on your Google Calendar, sends a confirmation
- **E-commerce order support** — Customer asks "where is my order?" AI looks up the order in your data table and replies with the status

## Quick glossary

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Workflow** | The complete automation you build — all the blocks connected together |
| **Block** | A single step in a workflow (send a message, make a decision, look up data) |
| **Trigger** | The event that starts a workflow (customer sends a message, someone calls) |
| **Channel** | How you communicate — WhatsApp, voice, email, or SMS |
| **Session** | One customer's conversation with your AI, from start to finish |
| **Data Table** | A spreadsheet-style table inside Lynq where you store your business data (rooms, products, listings) |
| **Industry Pack** | A pre-built workflow bundle for a specific industry, installed in one click |
