## Hospitality: Resort Booking on WhatsApp

**Business:** Beach resort in Goa with Standard, Deluxe, and Sea Villa room types  
**Goal:** Guest messages WhatsApp → AI checks availability → quotes price → confirms booking

### The workflow

```
Inbound WhatsApp (Trigger)
    ↓
Intent Classifier (AI)
    ├─ ROOM_INQUIRY → Room booking path
    ├─ SPA → Spa booking path
    ├─ RESTAURANT → Restaurant path
    ├─ EXISTING_GUEST → Guest services path
    └─ COMPLAINT → Escalate immediately
```

**Room booking path:**

```
AI Conversation
  Goal: "Ask for check-in date, check-out date, number of guests, 
         and room type preference. Confirm once you have all four."
    ↓
Get Table Row (Data Tables → Rooms)
  Filter: type = {{collected.room_type}}, 
          max_guests >= {{collected.guests}},
          available = Yes
  Sort: price ascending
    ↓
Branch: Room found?
  YES:
    → AI Conversation
        Goal: "Tell the guest about the available room including price per night,
               total for their stay, and amenities. Ask them to confirm."
    → Create Appointment (books dates on calendar)
    → Update Table Row (mark room as unavailable)
    → Create Deal (stage: "Booking Confirmed")
    → Send WhatsApp: "Booking confirmed! See you on {{checkin_date}}. 
                      Booking ref: {{deal.id}}"
    → Alert Team: "New booking: {{room_name}}, {{checkin_date}} to {{checkout_date}},
                   {{guests}} guests. Guest: {{contact.name}} {{contact.phone}}"
  NO:
    → AI Conversation
        Goal: "Apologise that the requested room is not available for those dates.
               Suggest alternative room types from: {{available_alternatives}}.
               Or offer to add them to a waitlist."
```

### What the conversation looks like

> **Guest:** Hi, do you have a sea villa available for 4 people from July 15-18?  
> **AI:** Hello! We'd love to host you. Just to confirm — you're looking for a Sea Villa for 4 guests checking in July 15 and checking out July 18, that's 3 nights. Is that right?  
> **Guest:** Yes exactly  
> **AI:** Great news! Sea Villa B is available for those dates. It's ₹18,000 per night — ₹54,000 for 3 nights. It includes a private pool, sea-facing terrace, BBQ area, and daily breakfast. Shall I confirm the booking?  
> **Guest:** Yes please  
> **AI:** Your booking is confirmed! Booking reference: #4821. You'll receive a detailed confirmation shortly. We look forward to welcoming you on July 15th!

## Real Estate: Lead Qualification + Auto-Scoring

**Business:** Residential real estate broker in Mumbai  
**Goal:** WhatsApp inquiry → AI qualifies lead → scores and routes hot/warm/cold → alerts broker for hot leads

### The workflow

```
Inbound WhatsApp (Trigger)
    ↓
AI Conversation (AI)
  Goal: "Qualify this lead with BANT:
         B - Budget: What is their total budget?
         A - Authority: Are they the sole decision maker?
         N - Need: What exactly are they looking for? (area, bedrooms, type)
         T - Timeline: When do they plan to purchase?
         
         Also collect: are they NRI? Looking for investment or self-use?
         
         Be conversational — don't ask all questions at once."
    ↓
Lead Scorer (AI)
  Scoring criteria:
    Budget ≥ ₹1Cr: +25 points
    Clear Authority (sole DM): +20 points
    Specific Need (area + bedrooms stated): +20 points
    Timeline ≤ 3 months: +25 points
    NRI or investment buyer: +10 points
    ↓
Branch: Score?
  ≥ 70 (HOT):
    → Create Deal (pipeline: "Sales", stage: "Hot Lead")
    → Update Contact (custom fields: budget, location, timeline, lead_score)
    → Alert Team: "🔥 HOT LEAD — {{contact.name}} ({{contact.phone}})
                   Budget: {{collected.budget}}
                   Looking for: {{collected.property_need}}
                   Timeline: {{collected.timeline}}
                   Score: {{lead_score}}/100
                   Call within 15 minutes!"
    → Send WhatsApp: "Thank you {{contact.first_name}}! One of our senior 
                      advisors will call you shortly to discuss properties 
                      that match your requirements."
                      
  40–69 (WARM):
    → Create Deal (stage: "Warm Lead")
    → Send WhatsApp: "Thank you! I've noted your requirements. 
                      We'll share some matching properties with you shortly."
    → Wait 1 day
    → Content Generator: "Based on {{collected.location}}, {{collected.bedrooms}}, 
                           {{collected.budget}}, write a personalised WhatsApp message 
                           sharing 2-3 property highlights we have available."
    → Send WhatsApp (generated message)
    → Wait 3 days → repeat follow-up...
    
  < 40 (COLD):
    → Create Deal (stage: "Cold Lead")
    → Send WhatsApp: "Thank you for reaching out! We'll keep you updated 
                      as new properties come to market that match what you're looking for."
    → Add to 90-day reactivation sequence
```

## Healthcare: After-Hours Patient Intake

**Business:** Multi-specialty clinic  
**Goal:** Patient calls after business hours → AI takes their details → books next available slot

### The workflow

```
Inbound Call (Trigger)
    ↓
Branch: Is it business hours?
  YES → Hand off to receptionist
  NO:
    ↓
    Start Voice Call (AI answering machine / after-hours mode)
      Persona: "Friendly receptionist, empathetic, clear speech"
      Goal: "Inform the patient the clinic is currently closed.
             Ask for their name, phone number, reason for visit, 
             and preferred appointment time (morning/afternoon/specific date).
             Reassure them the team will call back first thing tomorrow."
    ↓
    Branch: Emergency keywords detected?
      (chest pain, unconscious, accident, can't breathe, severe bleeding)
      YES:
        → Send SMS: "Emergency? Please call 108 or go to the nearest hospital immediately."
        → End Call
      NO:
        → Create Contact (name, phone, reason, preferred_time)
        → Create Deal (pipeline: "Appointments", stage: "After-Hours Request")
        → Alert Team: "After-hours call: {{contact.name}} ({{contact.phone}})
                       Reason: {{collected.reason}}
                       Preferred time: {{collected.preferred_time}}
                       Call back first thing tomorrow."
        → Send SMS to patient: "Your request has been logged. 
                                 Our team will call you first thing tomorrow at {{contact.phone}}."
```

## E-commerce: Order Status + Returns

**Business:** Online clothing store  
**Goal:** Customer messages WhatsApp → AI detects intent → handles order lookup or return request

### The workflow

```
Inbound WhatsApp (Trigger)
    ↓
Intent Classifier (AI)
    ├─ ORDER_STATUS → Order lookup path
    ├─ RETURN_REQUEST → Returns path
    ├─ PRODUCT_QUESTION → Knowledge base search
    └─ OTHER → AI Conversation (general)
```

**Order Status path:**
```
AI Conversation
  Goal: "Ask for their order ID or phone number to look up their order."
    ↓
HTTP Request
  URL: https://api.yourstore.com/v1/orders/lookup
  Body: { "identifier": "{{collected.order_id_or_phone}}" }
  Save as: order_data
    ↓
Branch: Order found?
  YES:
    → Send WhatsApp: 
      "Here's your order status:
       Order #{{order_data.id}} — {{order_data.status}}
       Items: {{order_data.items_summary}}
       {{order_data.tracking_url ? 'Track your order: ' + order_data.tracking_url : ''}}"
  NO:
    → Send WhatsApp: "I couldn't find an order with that ID. 
                      Could you double-check the order number? 
                      It's in your confirmation email subject line."
```

**Returns path:**
```
AI Conversation
  Goal: "Collect their order ID and the reason for return 
         (wrong size/damaged/changed mind/other)."
    ↓
Create Ticket
  Subject: "Return request — Order {{collected.order_id}}"
  Details: "Reason: {{collected.return_reason}} | Customer: {{contact.phone}}"
  Priority: {{collected.reason == 'damaged' ? 'high' : 'normal'}}
    ↓
Send WhatsApp: 
  "Your return request has been logged (Ticket #{{ticket.id}}). 
   Our team will reach out within 24 hours with return instructions. 
   {{collected.reason == 'damaged' ? 'Since your item arrived damaged, we will prioritise your case.' : ''}}"
```

## HR: Job Application + Interview Scheduling

**Business:** IT staffing firm  
**Goal:** Candidate messages WhatsApp about a job opening → AI collects profile → books interview

### The workflow

```
Inbound WhatsApp (Trigger)
    ↓
Intent Classifier (AI)
    ├─ JOB_APPLICATION → Candidate path
    └─ EMPLOYER_INQUIRY → Client path
```

**Candidate path:**
```
AI Conversation
  Goal: "Collect: (1) candidate's full name, (2) role they're applying for, 
         (3) years of experience, (4) current CTC, (5) expected CTC, 
         (6) notice period, (7) current location, (8) skills summary.
         
         Also ask them to share their resume as a document or Google Drive link."
    ↓
Get Table Row (Job Openings table)
  Filter: role matches {{collected.role}}, status = Active
    ↓
Branch: Matching opening found?
  YES:
    → Create Contact (candidate profile)
    → Create Deal (pipeline: "Candidates", stage: "Applied")
    → Check Calendar (recruiter availability for next 3 days)
    → AI Conversation
        Goal: "Present 2-3 available interview slots. Collect their preference."
    → Create Appointment (interview slot)
    → Send WhatsApp: 
        "Interview confirmed for {{interview.datetime}} via {{interview.mode}}.
         {{interview.mode == 'video' ? 'Meeting link: ' + interview.link : 'Location: ' + interview.address}}
         Please keep your resume ready. All the best!"
    → Alert Team: "New candidate: {{contact.name}} for {{collected.role}}
                   Interview: {{interview.datetime}}
                   CTC: {{collected.current_ctc}} → {{collected.expected_ctc}}
                   Notice: {{collected.notice_period}}"
  NO:
    → Send WhatsApp: 
        "We don't have an active opening for {{collected.role}} right now. 
         I've saved your profile and we'll reach out as soon as a matching 
         opportunity comes up!"
    → Create Contact (flag: "passive_candidate")
```

## Key patterns to reuse

These patterns appear across almost every industry — learn them once and apply them everywhere.

### The "collect and qualify" pattern

```
AI Conversation (collect info)
    → Lead Scorer
    → Branch on score
        HOT: Alert Team immediately
        WARM: Drip sequence
        COLD: CRM + reactivation
```

### The "look up and quote" pattern

```
AI Conversation (collect criteria)
    → Get Table Row (filter on criteria)
    → Branch: found?
        YES: AI Conversation (present options)
        NO: AI Conversation (offer alternatives or waitlist)
```

### The "book and confirm" pattern

```
AI Conversation (collect preferred time)
    → Check Calendar (availability)
    → AI Conversation (present slots, get confirmation)
    → Create Appointment
    → Send WhatsApp (confirmation)
    → Alert Team
    → Wait → Send reminder T-24h → Send reminder T-2h
```

### The "after-hours" pattern

```
Branch: Is current time within business hours?
    YES: Continue main workflow
    NO: Send WhatsApp ("We're closed. Back at 9am.")
        → Create Contact / Create Ticket
        → Alert Team (morning notification)
```

### The "escalate on sentiment" pattern

```
Sentiment Analyzer
    → Branch: sentiment = negative?
        YES: 
            → Add tag: "complaint"
            → Alert Team: "Customer complaint — {{contact.name}}: {{message_summary}}"
            → Assign to Agent (senior support)
            → Send WhatsApp: "We're sorry to hear that. A senior team member will reach out within the hour."
        NO: Continue normal flow
```
