The AI Workflow Generator lets you skip the canvas entirely for your first workflow. Describe what you want in plain English — Lynq uses AI to generate the blocks, configure them, and lay out the workflow on the canvas. You then review and adjust.

---

## How it works

1. Click **Create Workflow** → **Generate with AI**
2. Describe your business and what you want to automate
3. Lynq generates the complete workflow
4. Review the blocks on the canvas, adjust anything, and publish

The generator doesn't just create a generic template — it reads your description and configures each block's settings specifically for what you described.

---

## How to write a good prompt

The better your description, the better the result. You don't need to be technical — just describe your business the way you'd explain it to a new hire.

**Include these things:**
- What kind of business you run
- Which channel triggers the workflow (WhatsApp, phone call, etc.)
- What information you want to collect from the customer
- What happens at the end (booking, deal in your CRM, human handoff)
- Any routing logic ("if they want a refund, escalate to human")

## Example prompts

### Resort / hotel booking

```
I run a beach resort in Goa. When a customer messages my WhatsApp, 
I want my AI to ask about their preferred dates, number of guests, 
and room type (we have Standard, Deluxe, and Sea Villa). 

Check our room availability table and quote the price. 
If they confirm, book the dates, create a deal in my CRM pipeline, 
and alert my reservations team on WhatsApp.

If they're asking about spa or restaurant, collect their preferences 
and forward to the relevant team.
```

### Real estate lead qualification

```
I'm a real estate broker. When a lead comes in through WhatsApp, 
I want the AI to qualify them with BANT questions:
- Budget range
- Area/location preference  
- Type of property (apartment, villa, commercial)
- Timeline (ready to move vs. investing)
- Authority (are they the decision maker)

Score the lead. If HOT, create a deal and alert me immediately so 
I can call them within 15 minutes. If WARM, add them to a 7-day 
drip sequence. If COLD, just log in CRM with a follow-up task.
```

### Medical clinic

```
I run a dental clinic. When a patient calls after hours, the AI 
should take their name, phone number, and preferred appointment 
time. Check my Google Calendar for availability and offer 2-3 slots. 

Once they confirm, book it and send a WhatsApp confirmation. 
Send them a reminder the day before and 2 hours before.

If they mention tooth pain or emergency, skip the booking and 
give them our emergency number immediately.
```

### E-commerce customer support

```
When a customer messages our WhatsApp, detect if they're asking 
about an order status, a return, or a product question.

For order status: ask for their order ID, look it up in our 
Shopify store, and reply with the current status.

For returns: collect their order ID and reason, create a support 
ticket, and tell them our team will reach out in 24 hours.

For product questions: search our knowledge base and answer.
```

## After generation

The generator creates a complete workflow on the canvas. Before publishing:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review each block">
    Click each block and read its configuration. The AI sometimes uses generic text — replace it with your actual business name, hours, and specific details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test in the preview">
    Click **Test Workflow** to simulate a conversation. Try a few different paths — a happy path (normal customer), a confused customer, and a complaint.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your data">
    If the workflow uses Data Tables or calendar lookups, make sure those are connected and populated before publishing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Click **Publish** when you're ready. The workflow goes live immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Editing a generated workflow

The generated workflow is fully editable — it's identical to a workflow you'd build by hand. You can:

- Add, remove, or reorder blocks
- Change any block's configuration
- Add new branches and conditions
- Connect integrations

The AI generator just gives you a starting point. Final control is always yours.

---

## Limitations

The AI generator works best for workflows up to ~15 blocks. For very complex multi-agent workflows (lead qualification → sales call → drip sequence → CRM update → renewal) — consider using an [Industry Pack](/workflow-builder/industry-packs) which comes pre-built and tested for your industry.

<Tip>
You can use the generator iteratively. Generate a simple version first, publish it, see how customers interact with it, then use the generator again with more specific requirements to improve it.
</Tip>

## What the AI can and can't configure automatically

| The AI will configure | You need to do manually |
|---|---|
| Block types and order | Connect your WhatsApp account |
| AI Conversation goals and personas | Connect Google Calendar |
| Branch conditions | Add your products to Data Tables |
| Message templates with variable placeholders | Enter your CRM API credentials |
| Lead scoring criteria | Upload your knowledge base documents |
| Routing logic | Set your business hours |

The generator builds the logic skeleton — you connect your real accounts and data.
