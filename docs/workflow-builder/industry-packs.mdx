## What is an Industry Pack?

An Industry Pack is a ready-made bundle of everything you need to run AI automation for a specific type of business — pre-configured automations, a pipeline with the right stages, an AI brain trained on your industry's terminology, and a setup wizard that personalises it to your business in 5 minutes.

Instead of building blocks from scratch, you answer a few questions about your business and Lynq installs everything automatically.

## How to install a pack

1. Go to **WhatsApp → Settings → Industry Packs**
2. Browse the available packs and click **Install** on the one that matches your business
3. Complete the 5-step setup wizard (takes about 5 minutes)
4. Click **Install Pack** — Lynq creates everything automatically
5. Your AI is live and ready to handle customer conversations

### What gets created on install

| Created automatically | You provide in the wizard |
|---|---|
| 10–15 pre-built automations | Your business name and location |
| Pipeline with industry-specific stages | Your room/service/product types |
| AI system prompt trained on your industry | Your business hours and timezone |
| Knowledge base template with FAQs | After-hours message |
| Welcome and confirmation message templates | Escalation contact (your phone/email) |

## Hospitality Pack {#hospitality-pack}

**For:** Hotels, beach resorts, hill resorts, heritage properties, homestays, boutique properties

### What the AI handles

The AI classifies every incoming message into one of these intents and routes accordingly:

| Intent | AI behaviour |
|---|---|
| **Room inquiry** | Collects dates, guests, room type preference. Queries your Rooms data table. Quotes price and availability. |
| **Booking confirmation** | Confirms reservation details. Creates deal. Sends booking confirmation. |
| **Spa / wellness** | Collects preferences. Checks availability. Books slot. |
| **Restaurant reservation** | Collects date, time, party size, occasion. Creates reservation record. |
| **Activities / experiences** | Collects interest and dates. Books activity or escalates to activities team. |
| **Existing guest** | Looks up active booking. Answers questions about check-in, services, amenities. |
| **Complaint** | Logs complaint with sentiment. Escalates immediately to duty manager. |
| **VIP signal** | Detects NRI, anniversary, honeymoon, corporate signals. Escalates to senior sales with full context. |

### Automations installed (13 pre-built)

| Automation | Trigger | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Welcome | First message | Greets customer, starts AI conversation |
| Booking confirmed | Keyword "booking confirmed" | Sends template + creates deal at "Booking Confirmed" stage |
| Pre-arrival D-7 | 7 days before check-in | Sends welcome info + upsells spa/dining |
| Pre-arrival reminder | 1 day before check-in | Sends check-in time, directions, contact |
| Check-in | Keyword "checked in" | Sends digital welcome pack, assigns to concierge |
| Restaurant inquiry | Keyword "restaurant" | AI handles reservation |
| Spa inquiry | Keyword "spa" | AI handles booking |
| Complaint handler | Negative sentiment detected | Acknowledges, escalates to duty manager |
| Post-stay review | 2 hours after checkout | Sends Google/TripAdvisor review request |
| Loyalty query | Keyword "points" | AI answers questions about your loyalty program |
| Late checkout request | Keyword "late checkout" | Checks availability, confirms or offers alternatives |
| Reactivation | 90 days inactive | Sends personalised offer to lapsed guest |
| Missed call recovery | Missed call → new message | "Sorry we missed your call. How can we help?" |

### Pipeline stages created

New Inquiry → Availability Checked → Booking Confirmed → Pre-Arrival → Checked In → Post-Stay

### Wizard questions

1. Property name, city, type (beach/hill/city/heritage/jungle)
2. Room/accommodation types with max capacity (add as many as you have)
3. Services offered: Spa, Restaurant, Pool, Activities, Events (toggle each + brief description)
4. Business hours, timezone, after-hours message
5. Escalation contact: duty manager phone, VIP contact phone

## Real Estate Pack {#real-estate-pack}

**For:** Residential brokers, property developers, commercial real estate, property management companies

### What the AI handles

| Intent | AI behaviour |
|---|---|
| **New inquiry** | BANT qualification — collects budget, location, property type, timeline, decision authority |
| **Specific property** | Looks up listing in your Properties data table. Shares price, area, availability. |
| **Site visit request** | Books site visit on Google Calendar. Sends confirmation. |
| **Document query** | Answers brochure/legal document questions from knowledge base. |
| **Existing client** | Looks up deal status. Updates with latest info from agent. |
| **Complaint** | Escalates immediately to principal broker with full context. |

### Lead scoring (BANT)

Each lead is automatically scored on 4 dimensions:

| Dimension | What the AI assesses |
|---|---|
| **Budget** | Does their stated budget match your property prices? |
| **Authority** | Are they the decision maker, or do they need spousal/family approval? |
| **Need** | Is their requirement specific and clearly articulated? |
| **Timeline** | Are they looking to buy in < 3 months, or just browsing? |

**HOT lead (score ≥ 70):** Instant notification to broker. Suggest calling within 15 minutes.  
**WARM lead (40–69):** Add to 7-day WhatsApp drip sequence.  
**COLD lead (< 40):** Log in CRM, add to 90-day reactivation sequence.

### Pipeline stages created

New Inquiry → Qualified → Site Visit Booked → Site Visit Done → Proposal Sent → Negotiation → Closed Won / Closed Lost

## Healthcare Pack {#healthcare-pack}

**For:** Multi-speciality clinics, dental practices, diagnostics, physiotherapy, wellness centres

### What the AI handles

| Intent | AI behaviour |
|---|---|
| **New patient** | Collects name, phone, reason for visit, preferred doctor, preferred slot |
| **Appointment booking** | Checks doctor availability, books slot on calendar, sends confirmation |
| **Existing patient** | Looks up upcoming appointment, answers questions about preparation |
| **Lab / diagnostics** | Explains test preparation, location, reporting time |
| **Emergency** | Immediately provides emergency contact, does not attempt to book |
| **Prescription query** | Escalates to doctor or nurse — AI never gives medical advice |
| **Billing** | Answers general billing questions, escalates for specific queries |

### Compliance built in

The Healthcare pack includes automatic compliance guardrails:
- AI never provides diagnosis or specific medical advice
- Every conversation starts with a privacy disclaimer
- Emergency signals (chest pain, unconscious, accident) immediately surface emergency contact
- Patient data is treated as PHI — agents see a "PHI Caution" flag on conversations

## Education Pack {#education-pack}

**For:** Coaching centres, ed-tech platforms, professional training, test prep institutes

### What the AI handles

| Intent | AI behaviour |
|---|---|
| **Course inquiry** | Collects student profile (qualification, goal, timeline). Recommends suitable course from your Courses table. |
| **Demo class booking** | Books a demo class slot. Sends joining link via WhatsApp. |
| **Fee / scholarship** | Answers fee structure questions from knowledge base. Mentions scholarship criteria. |
| **Enrollment** | Collects student details, creates enrollment record, sends payment link. |
| **Parent inquiry** | Adapts tone and content for parent vs. student conversations. |
| **Complaint / feedback** | Escalates to academic coordinator. |

### Automations installed

- Demo class reminder (T-24h and T-1h)
- Post-demo follow-up: "What did you think of the session?"
- Enrollment deadline reminder (3 days before batch starts)
- Fee reminder (7 days before due date)
- Parent progress report dispatch (monthly)

## Law Firm Pack {#law-firm-pack}

**For:** Individual advocates, law firms, legal consultancies, dispute resolution

### What the AI handles

| Intent | AI behaviour |
|---|---|
| **New client inquiry** | Collects case type, brief facts, urgency level |
| **Consultation booking** | Books initial consultation slot, sends calendar invite and pre-consultation form |
| **Document query** | Searches legal FAQ knowledge base for general questions |
| **Case status** | Escalates to assigned attorney — AI does not access case files |
| **Fee inquiry** | Provides general fee ranges from knowledge base, offers consultation for precise quote |
| **Emergency / urgent** | Flags conversation as urgent, notifies duty attorney |

### Compliance guardrails

- AI never provides legal advice — only general information
- Every conversation ends with: "This information is for general guidance only and does not constitute legal advice."
- Bar Council disclosure included in initial message if configured

## HR & Recruitment Pack {#hr-pack}

**For:** Staffing agencies, corporate HR teams, talent acquisition, placement consultancies

### What the AI handles

| Intent | AI behaviour |
|---|---|
| **Job inquiry** | Collects desired role, experience, location preference. Matches against your Job Openings table. |
| **Application** | Collects resume link, current CTC, notice period. Creates candidate record. |
| **Interview scheduling** | Books interview slot with recruiter. Sends confirmation with interview details. |
| **Status check** | Gives application status update. |
| **Employer inquiry** | Handles companies looking to fill positions. Collects hiring brief. Creates client lead. |

### Data Tables for HR

**Job Openings table:**
- Role, department, location, experience range, salary band, openings count, status (active/closed)

**Candidates table** (auto-populated from conversations):
- Name, phone, email, role applied for, current CTC, notice period, status, interview date

## Customising a pack after install

Installing a pack gives you a pre-configured starting point. You can customise every part of it:

- Edit any automation's trigger, steps, or messages
- Add new automations for scenarios the pack doesn't cover
- Modify the AI's system prompt to match your exact tone and brand
- Update Data Tables as your inventory changes
- Add or remove pipeline stages

<Warning>
Uninstalling a pack removes the automations and pipeline stages it created. Any deals already in the pipeline stages will remain, but the stages will be gone. Export your pipeline data before uninstalling.
</Warning>

## Running multiple packs

Each organisation can only have one active Industry Pack at a time. If your business spans multiple industries (e.g. a company that does both real estate and property management), contact support — we can configure a custom setup.
